# Pre-registration: does the Paper 1–4 forecasting stack predict astronomy?

Written 2026-10-15, before any question in either cohort was generated.
It is committed and pushed together with the frozen predictions, and before
any outcome label exists. The git history on GitHub timestamps that order.

## What is being tested

Papers 1–4 together make a forecasting claim: from the literature available
on a cutoff date, you can say which astronomy research questions the field
will substantively take up over the next ~5 years (Paper 3). You can also say
what evidence step should come next for a question (Paper 4).

- Paper 3 measured this only on cutoffs 2012–2020.
- Every coefficient in Paper 3 was fit on those cutoffs.
- The 2020 test labels end 2025-12-31.

Nothing has yet tested the stack on a cutoff it has never seen. This study
does that, then issues a forecast that only the future can grade.

| Paper | Role here |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — evidence-graph question discovery | the evidence-tension generator (Paper 3's `evidence_tension` source) |
| 2 — historical backtesting protocol | freeze → temporal isolation → outcome taxonomy; the 2026 prospective instance is scored here too |
| 3 — outcome prediction indicators | the predictor (features, models, labels), used unchanged |
| 4 — evidence planner | next-action recommendations; utility as a candidate predictor |

## Study 1: out-of-time backtest at cutoff 2021-12-31

### Cohort

- Paper 3's generators, quotas, prompts and subfield classifier are used
  unchanged: 8 subfields × (10 tension, 5 direct-LLM, 5 future-work,
  5 control).
- Past window: 2014-01-01 to 2021-12-31.
- Questions are generated by `gpt-4o-mini`, as in Paper 3.

### Outcome window

- 2022-01-01 to 2026-10-14, which is 4.79 years. Paper 3 used 5 years.
- The arXiv snapshot was harvested on 2026-10-15.

### Predictors

All predictors are frozen before labelling.

**Primary: `m_logistic__asof2021`**
- Paper 3's structured logistic model, trained on Paper 3's cutoffs
  2012/2014/2016 only.
- Those labels end by 2021-12-31, so a forecaster could have fit this
  model on the cutoff day. That makes it free of outcome-window leakage.

**Secondary models**
- `m_logistic__all`: trained on all 980 questions. It is the model used for
  the 2026 forecast. Its 2018/2020 training labels overlap this outcome
  window, so its backtest score is optimistic by construction.
- `m_gbm__*`: Paper 3's gradient-boosting model.

**Baselines** (each fitted on the same training set as the model it is
compared with)
- constant
- `b_environment`: subfield popularity
- `b_source`: training addressed-rate of the generation source
- `b_text`: TF-IDF of the question text

**Other candidate predictors** (tension questions only)
- `s_structure`: Paper 3's evidence-graph structure prior
- Paper 4's planner top-k utility, under the `default` and `paper3_informed`
  configs

### Outcome labels

**L1 (primary).** Paper 3's label, unchanged:
- TF-IDF retrieval of the top 8 future papers in the subfield
- the `gpt-4o-mini` strict judge
- `addressed` = at least one direct candidate and an outcome other than
  `not_addressed`

L1 is the label Paper 3's model was trained to predict.

**L2.** The same candidates re-judged by `gpt-4o`, Paper 3's second judge,
for every question.

**L3 (Paper 4's correction).**
- Paper 4 showed that abstract retrieval misses most engagement with
  narrow questions.
- L3 adds candidates that cite the question's source papers (citation graph
  from Semantic Scholar) and that were published in the outcome window.
- The top 8 by text similarity are judged by the L1 judge.
- `addressed_L3 = addressed_L1 OR addressed_citation`.
- Questions without source papers (direct-LLM, vague controls) keep L1.

### Hypotheses

Each hypothesis is evaluated on L1. Each is also reported on L2 and L3.

| ID | Hypothesis | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | The primary model discriminates out of time | AUC > 0.5 with the 95% bootstrap CI (2,000 resamples, seed 7) excluding 0.5 |
| H2 | It beats the cheap baselines | paired-bootstrap ΔAUC 95% CI > 0 vs `b_environment` **and** vs `b_source` |
| H3 | It discriminates within generation source, not only between sources | mean within-source AUC > 0.5, with the bootstrap CI excluding 0.5 |
| H4 | Its probabilities are usable | Brier score below the training-base-rate constant's |
| H5 | Paper 3's structure prior predicts within tension questions | AUC > 0.5, CI excluding 0.5 |
| H6 | Paper 4's planner utility does **not** predict attention (Paper 4 withdrew that claim) | two-sided: the CI on AUC contains 0.5 ⇒ replicated null |
| H7 | Paper 4's top-ranked action type matches how the field actually followed up | top-1 match rate above a permutation null (the planner's top-1 shuffled across questions, 5,000 permutations, one-sided p < 0.05) |

### H7 measurement

- The population is tension questions that are addressed under L3.
- `gpt-4o` classifies the evidence step taken by each direct follow-up paper
  into Paper 4's action catalog: collect observation, rerun analysis,
  compare datasets, run simulation, search archive, or review.
- A match is when the planner's top-1 action type appears among those types.

### Reporting rules

- All hypotheses are reported, whatever the outcome.
- No predictor, feature, prompt or threshold changes after the freeze.
- Any deviation is listed in the results with its reason.

## Study 2: prospective forecast, cutoff 2026-10-14

### Cohort

- A new Paper-3-style cohort: 8 subfields × 25 questions, past window
  2018-10-15 to 2026-10-14.
- Paper 2's 200 questions frozen on 2026-08-17, scored with the same model
  at their own cutoff.

### Forecast content

- `p_attention`: the probability that the question is substantively
  addressed within 5 years, from `m_logistic__all`.
- Paper 4's ranked next-evidence actions, for tension questions.

### Freeze and scoring

- Everything is frozen with SHA-256 digests in `FREEZE.json`.
- The score window is 2026-10-15 to 2031-10-14, using the L1 protocol.
- Scoring may run no earlier than 2031-10-15.
- Paper 2's instance keeps its own window, 2027–2030.

### Prospective claims

These are the claims that will be checked at scoring time.

- **P1:** AUC of `p_attention` against L1 outcomes is > 0.5.
- **P2:** The top-quintile `p_attention` questions are addressed at a higher
  rate than the bottom quintile.
